# Recommended Pages & Project Structure

## Smart Funding Wizard
> A multi-step, frictionless application experience designed to qualify DC businesses while maintaining a premium fintech aesthetic. It collects business vitals, revenue data, and specific funding needs using conditional logic to provide immediate feedback on eligibility.

### Suggested Files:
- `WizardContainer.jsx`
- `BusinessDetailsForm.jsx`
- `FinancialSnapshot.jsx`
- `DocumentUploader.jsx`
- `ReviewAndSubmit.jsx`
- `WizardStyles.module.css`
