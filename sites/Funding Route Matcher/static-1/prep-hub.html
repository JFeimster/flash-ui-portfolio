Foundation & Compliance

Entity Registration

Business Physical Address

Professional Email & Domain

02

Financial Hygiene

Dedicated Business Account

Separate all personal and business transactions immediately.

Clean 3-Month Trajectory

Maintain average daily balances above $1,000 for 90 days.

03

Credit Building

D&B Profile Setup

Claim your DUNS number and ensure industry codes (NAICS) are correct.

Trade Line Activation

Open 3 Net-30 accounts (Uline, Grainger, etc.) to report payment history.

Quick Tips

Lender Optics

Banks use AI to scan your bank statements for "NSF" (Non-Sufficient Funds). Ensure zero bounce-backs.

NAICS Codes

Certain codes are "high risk" (e.g., Credit Repair). Ensure yours accurately reflects your business.
