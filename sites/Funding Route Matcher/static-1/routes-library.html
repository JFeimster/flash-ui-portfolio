Max Limit $500,000
Difficulty Moderate

Working Capital

Standard unsecured business funding designed for operational expansion, marketing, or general liquidity. This is the "Gold Standard" for established businesses with consistent revenue streams and dedicated business banking.

Primary Requirements
  • Registered LLC/Corp
  • 6+ Months in Business
  • $15k+ Monthly Revenue
  • Business Checking Account
  • Clean NSF History
  • 600+ Credit Score

Quick Micro-Funding

Optimized for gig-economy workers, solopreneurs, and micro-businesses. This route relies heavily on real-time bank data (Plaid) rather than credit scores or lengthy tax documentation.

Primary Requirements
  • 3 Months Bank History
  • Active Personal/Gig Acct
  • $3k+ Monthly Deposits
  • Valid Gov. ID
  • US Citizenship/Residency
  • Instant Data Connection

Speed 7 - 14 Days
Max Limit $5,000,000+
Difficulty Asset-Based

Real Estate Funding

Bridge loans and fix-and-flip financing for investors. Unlike traditional business loans, the primary underwriting criteria is the After Repair Value (ARV) and the property's specific equity position.

Primary Requirements
  • Property Address
  • HUD Settlement Table
  • Rehab/Project Budget
  • Track Record (Preferred)
  • Entity Formation Docs
  • 640+ Mid-Score

Speed 3 - 5 Days
Max Limit Invoice Total
Difficulty Low (Secured)

Equipment Financing

Secured financing for vehicles, heavy machinery, or tech hardware. Because the equipment serves as collateral, terms are often longer (up to 72 months) and interest rates are significantly lower than unsecured lines.

Primary Requirements
  • Vendor Quote/Invoice
  • 2 Years Tax Returns
  • Insurance Proof
  • 660+ Credit Score
  • Entity in Good Standing
  • Down Payment (5-10%)

Business Credit Prep

For applicants who don't yet meet the criteria for institutional capital. This is a strategic "rebuild" lane focused on establishing trade lines, fixing entity data mismatches, and improving the personal-to-business credit bridge.

Focus Areas
  • D&B / Experian Biz Profile
  • Tier 1 Trade Vendors
  • Entity Address Cleanup
  • Personal Credit Repair
  • P&L Optimization
