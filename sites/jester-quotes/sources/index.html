Behind the curtain / Provenance

Credit where it's due.

A collection can celebrate the fool and still be serious about attribution. Here is what the registry actually documents—and what it does not.

01 / Collection source

One supplied collection.

The 100-quotation registry derives from 100 Jester Quotes - Know Your Archetypes. The original source link is provided across the site.

Author names and available reference fields are preserved as provided; they have not been independently verified for every quotation.

03 / Editorial organization

Themes, not new attributions.

The 6 themed collections are editorial groupings created for discovery. A quotation may appear in more than one theme; those labels are not original source metadata and do not establish what an author intended.

Explore the themes ↗

04 / Reuse & correction

Attribution isn't a license.

Credit to an author or source does not establish permission to reproduce third-party text. Republishing, printing, merchandising, or wider redistribution may require rights clearance or another valid legal basis.

Found a misattribution or correction? Submit an issue on GitHub with the quotation number and supporting source. No corrections are automatically applied to the original registry.

05 / Trickster directory

Independent reference notes.

The Trickster Directory contains 11 short original profiles backed by external references. It is a curated introduction, not a reproduction of another researcher's list or a claim that every culture interprets these figures alike.

Aadip Desai’s Trickster Hit List! contains 139 numbered research leads and is credited as inspiration, not reproduced. A separate unpublished research-candidate CSV is maintained. No third-party character pictures were copied.

06 / Linked resources and video catalog

Original sources stay original.

The Resource Vault has 26 independently linked references and the Video Library features 87 verified YouTube links. All summaries are written for this site, and the original publishers retain their content.

Resource cards link outward to their original pages. Video embeds are not loaded until a visitor chooses to load the YouTube player; external video links work without embedding. Publisher inclusion does not imply endorsement or grant any media reuse permission.

Original quotation records remain in the public source registry. The corresponding credit policy is in CREDITS.md.
