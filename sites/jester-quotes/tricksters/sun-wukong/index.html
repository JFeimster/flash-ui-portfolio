Sun Wukong (Monkey King)

Chinese literary tradition, notably Journey to the West

LiteratureLiteratureMythology
✳NO UNLICENSED ARTWORK

The story behind the mask

Beyond the label.

The Monkey King of Journey to the West changes form, resists authority and seeks freedom from limits. His fantastic abilities make him both an unruly figure and a formidable traveling companion.
