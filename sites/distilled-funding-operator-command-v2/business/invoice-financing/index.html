DF // QUESTIONS 06

ASK BEFORE THE PAYMENT SCHEDULE ANSWERS FOR YOU.

What is invoice / accounts receivable financing?

Invoice / Accounts Receivable Financing is a financing category or use case that should be evaluated against the borrower's purpose, repayment source, qualification profile, and timing. The exact structure varies by product and provider.

How do I know if invoice / accounts receivable financing fits my situation?

Start with what the money is for, how quickly it is needed, what supports repayment, and which parts of the file are strongest. A good fit is a structure the business or borrower can realistically carry.

What usually affects qualification?

Credit, revenue or income, time in business, documentation, requested amount, existing obligations, collateral or asset strength, and the specific funding lane can all matter.

How fast can funding happen?

Timing depends on the structure, documentation, underwriting, and file quality. Some lanes are designed for speed; others trade speed for longer terms or more structured underwriting.
