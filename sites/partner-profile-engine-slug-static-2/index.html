+

Does submitting a funding review guarantee approval?

+

No. Every profile requires full underwriting verification. However, performing this diagnostic early allows [PARTNER_NAME] to locate vulnerabilities, clean up preparation issues, and match you to highly optimized pipelines, maximizing actual performance parameters.

What documents should I prepare?

+

At a baseline: 3 months of business bank statements (PDFs), your corporate tax EIN certificate, and a valid driver's license. Real Estate and Equipment options may request additional specs, including invoice parameters or property deal structures.

Do I need a business bank account?

+

Yes. Direct commercial underwriters require a verifiable business bank account to clear funds. Accounts operating under private personal ledgers are heavily limited or rejected. We can support structuring business setups if you are non-compliant today.

Can gig workers or self-employed people use this?

+

Yes. If you have clean proof of independent income (such as stripe dashboards, 1099 contracts, or regular ledger updates) we have unique paths explicitly geared towards sole-proprietor and gig setups.

How fast can funding happen?

+

Working capital files are routinely cleared and distributed in 24 to 48 hours. Complex property projects and massive logistics gear operations take slightly longer due to physical collateral verifications.

What if I am not ready yet?

+

That is exactly why this portal exists. You can download the checklists, run the readiness score tools, or book a call to review parameters. We work closely to lay out clear 90-day positioning paths to ensure you hit eligibility limits.
