# Recommended Pages & Project Structure

## Vetting & Application Flow
> A multi-step, interactive questionnaire designed to filter applicants and reinforce exclusivity. Using brutalist borders and high-contrast typography, it collects financial goals, experience, and lead-gen capacity to ensure only high-quality partners apply.

### Suggested Files:
- `ApplicationLayout.tsx`
- `StepIndicator.tsx`
- `QuestionCard.tsx`
- `QualifyingLogic.ts`
- `ApplicationForm.module.css`

---

## Application Success & 'The Blueprint' Video
> A high-energy post-submission page that confirms receipt and immediately provides a 'Quick Start' training video. It maintains the punk fintech aesthetic with electric green highlights and clear, punchy next steps to reduce drop-off while the application is reviewed.

### Suggested Files:
- `SuccessHero.tsx`
- `TrainingVideoPlayer.tsx`
- `NextStepsTimeline.tsx`
- `OnboardingEmailTrigger.ts`
- `SuccessPage.css`

---

## Partner Command Center (Dashboard)
> The main hub for approved partners to track their agency's progress. It features a dark-mode, data-heavy layout with white cards displaying lead status, funding volume, and real-time commission earnings with brutalist chart visualizations.

### Suggested Files:
- `DashboardLayout.tsx`
- `EarningsOverview.tsx`
- `LeadTrackerTable.tsx`
- `CommissionChart.tsx`
- `PartnerStats.api.ts`

---

## The Vault (Resource & Script Library)
> A searchable, categorized repository of sales scripts, email templates, and marketing assets. It uses a card-based grid with bold borders and energetic visual hierarchy to make finding 'The Machine's' components effortless for new partners.

### Suggested Files:
- `ResourceVault.tsx`
- `ScriptCard.tsx`
- `AssetFilterBar.tsx`
- `DownloadManager.tsx`
- `VaultData.json`

---

## Agency Income Projection Tool
> An interactive calculator that allows partners to visualize their income potential. Users can adjust sliders for lead volume and average funding amounts, with the UI dynamically updating 'potential payouts' in real-time using electric green typography.

### Suggested Files:
- `IncomeCalculator.tsx`
- `ProjectionSlider.tsx`
- `RevenueBreakdown.tsx`
- `CalculationLogic.ts`
- `CalculatorStyles.module.css`
