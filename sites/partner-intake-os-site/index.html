THE OPERATING PROBLEM

The Problem Isn't More Partners.
It's Knowing What To Do With Them.

Partner pipelines get messy because fundamentally different opportunities land in the same bucket.

a CPA with 300 business clients a first-time affiliate a broker sending active deals a business attorney a creator with an audience a veteran organization a fintech integration opportunity a random lead seller

They should not receive the same follow-up, resources, onboarding, campaign, CRM treatment, or priority.

Partner Intake OS turns “someone filled out the partner form” into an actual operating decision.

FAQ

What Partner Intake OS Actually Is.

What is Partner Intake OS?+

An AI-powered partner operations assistant that converts partner inquiries and signup information into structured classifications, tiers, onboarding recommendations, CRM-ready data, campaign ideas, and next actions.

Who is it designed for?+

Operators managing brokers, referral partners, affiliates, professional centers of influence, community partners, creators, fintech relationships, and strategic partnerships.

Does it approve partners automatically?+

No. It can recommend classifications and flag issues, but important partnership decisions can remain subject to human review.

What can I give it?+

Partner applications, intake submissions, notes, referral introductions, broker information, CRM data, or other relevant context.

What does it return?+

Depending on the request: classification, tier, scorecard, risk level, onboarding path, CRM fields, resources, campaign recommendations, next action, and structured partner records.

Is it only for funding brokers?+

No. The architecture supports multiple partner types including brokers, referral partners, affiliates, CPAs, attorneys, business brokers, creators, community organizations, fintech companies, and strategic partners.

Is this the same thing as submitting a funding application?+

No. Partner Intake OS evaluates partners and partner relationships. It should not confuse partner intake with borrower or funding-applicant underwriting.

Can it connect to other systems?+

The broader architecture is designed around API-based workflows that can support systems such as Tally, Vercel, HubSpot, Notion, Google Sheets, and a future Partner Command Center.
