The Anatomy of a Hard Money Transaction

From system ingestion to capital paydown. Follow the exact operational pipeline typical of asset-based financing protocols.

01

Asset Assessment

The hard money system targets the property's purchase cost, potential rehab requirements, and After Repair Value (ARV) over personal income metrics.

02

Parameter Origination

Lenders secure structural commitment metrics, issuing term sheets containing exact points, interest-only options, and loan limits.

03

Closing & Holdbacks

Funds close rapidly (often 5-14 days). Original purchase capital is distributed; construction reserves are escrowed in structured holdbacks.

04

Escrow Release Flow

Borrowers complete rehabilitation phases and trigger Draw Schedule protocols to recoup escrowed funds for ongoing project progress.

Tactical Playbook

Leverage Optimization Protocol

Hard money terms are rarely absolute. By structuring low-risk deal metrics and demonstrating deep experience, borrowers can renegotiate key levers such as origination points and exit penalty covenants.

Key Negotiable Targets

Origination Points

Typically range from 1% to 3%. Highly negotiable for repeat operators with clean execution histories, or on larger capitalization amounts.

Rehab Draw Fees

Lenders charge $150–$350 per site inspection/draw event. Request waivers of digital administrative draw fees or pre-arranged flat draw packages.

Prepayment Penalties

Protect your exit strategies. Negotiate "No Prepays" or minimize guaranteed minimum interest covenants if you anticipate rapid execution under 4 months.
