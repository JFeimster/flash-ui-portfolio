# Recommended Pages & Project Structure

## Performance Analytics Dashboard
> Provides a high-level visual summary of the referral ecosystem. It tracks key performance indicators such as lead conversion rates per referral category, total deal volume generated by partners, and a 'Heat Map' showing which professional niches (e.g., CPAs vs. Real Estate Agents) are currently most productive.

### Suggested Files:
- `app/dashboard/page.tsx`
- `components/analytics/MetricCard.tsx`
- `components/analytics/CategoryHeatMap.tsx`
- `components/charts/ConversionFunnel.tsx`
- `lib/analytics-utils.ts`

---

## Outreach Template Library
> A centralized repository for multi-channel communication scripts. Users can browse and customize pre-written outreach messages tailored to specific referral archetypes (e.g., 'The Bank Manager Intro' or 'The Accountant Value-Add'). Includes variables for auto-filling partner names and company details.

### Suggested Files:
- `app/templates/page.tsx`
- `components/templates/TemplateEditor.tsx`
- `components/templates/ScriptPreview.tsx`
- `components/shared/CopyButton.tsx`
- `hooks/useTemplateHydration.ts`

---

## Earnings & Commission Ledger
> Financial tracking page that connects closed business funding deals to their respective referral sources. It calculates commissions earned by the affiliate, shows pending versus cleared payouts, and provides a historical record of all revenue generated through the network.

### Suggested Files:
- `app/earnings/page.tsx`
- `components/earnings/PayoutTable.tsx`
- `components/earnings/CommissionSummary.tsx`
- `components/earnings/ExportReportButton.tsx`
- `types/earnings.ts`

---

## Partner Resource Hub
> An educational portal containing Moonshine Capital brand assets, pitch decks, and training videos. Designed to help affiliates speak confidently about funding products when meeting with sophisticated referral partners like Business Brokers or Consultants.

### Suggested Files:
- `app/resources/page.tsx`
- `app/resources/[slug]/page.tsx`
- `components/resources/VideoPlayer.tsx`
- `components/resources/AssetDownloader.tsx`
- `content/training-modules.json`

---

## Unified Follow-Up Calendar
> A specialized scheduling interface that aggregates all 'Next Follow-up' dates from the Referral Source Tracker. It provides a daily and weekly agenda view, allowing users to batch their relationship-building activities and ensure no high-priority partner is neglected.

### Suggested Files:
- `app/calendar/page.tsx`
- `components/calendar/FullCalendarView.tsx`
- `components/calendar/DailyTaskItem.tsx`
- `components/calendar/ReminderSettings.tsx`
- `hooks/useCalendarEvents.ts`
