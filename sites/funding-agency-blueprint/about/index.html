ABOUT / THE PUBLICATION

WE DON’T NEED ANOTHER FUNDING GURU.

The Funding Agency Blueprint is an independent editorial and implementation project for people building modern, responsible funding businesses.

Published by Moonshine Capital and authored by Jason Feimster, it combines practical business development, funding partner research, workflows, AI assistance and operator-level discipline.

It is not a lender, a funding offer, a certification program or a promise of income. Specific partner programs, licensing requirements, pricing and compliance obligations must be verified independently.
