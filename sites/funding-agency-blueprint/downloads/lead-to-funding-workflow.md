# Lead-to-Funding Workflow — Original template

1. Inquiry arrives. Record time, purpose and contact permission.
2. Validate consent and the minimum data permitted at this stage.
3. Create or find the CRM record idempotently.
4. Assign a human owner; schedule an acknowledgment.
5. Prepare a human-reviewed product/partner fit shortlist using current sources.
6. Request only required supporting data through authorized secure channels.
7. Record lender handoff and underwriting responsibility.
8. Track next action, response status and applicant consent.
9. Reconcile final outcome; avoid claims of guaranteed approval.
10. Perform allowed follow-up and document feedback.

This is educational and not a compliance checklist for any jurisdiction.
