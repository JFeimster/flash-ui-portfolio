PROCESS PATTERN / EDUCATIONAL

EXAMPLE: AN INTAKE THAT DOESN’T LEAK LEADS.

  1. Identify the requested financing purpose and obtain appropriate contact/data consent.
  2. Collect only required data at the current stage; avoid unnecessary sensitive uploads.
  3. Confirm business category, geography and available products with up-to-date partner rules.
  4. Assign an owner, record the status and schedule a concrete follow-up.
  5. Escalate underwriting, adverse-action and legal decisions to the appropriate responsible party.
