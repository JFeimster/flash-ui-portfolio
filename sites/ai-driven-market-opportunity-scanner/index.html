METHODOLOGY

How the ranking works

Each underlying event contributes once per opportunity and signal. A later report with the same event_key is still visible in the record, but does not multiply its influence.

Priority: 30% pain, 30% supporting spend, 20% buyer reach, and 20% timing. Each dimension is capped at 10. A supporting measured observation contributes 5–6 points; an anecdote contributes 3–4; an assumption contributes none. Contradictory claims are displayed, but do not add to priority.

Unknown: when a dimension lacks supporting non-assumption evidence, it stays “Unknown” and contributes no positive points. That is not equivalent to proving the dimension has a zero real-world value.

Confidence: a separate proxy based on the number and quality of unique supporting events, penalized for missing dimensions and offset by contradictions. All demo evidence is simulated and does not create genuine real-world confidence.

Decision: “Test” requires priority at least 45 and some supporting spend signal. “Watch” otherwise. “Pass” is reserved for a low-scoring hypothesis with direct contradictory buying evidence.
