Seller notes as acquisition financing buffers

When you need a buffer to satisfy senior debt underwriting or reduce cash down.

Build My Stack Submit Deal Back to lanes

Pros

  • Absorbs risk
  • Improves lender comfort
  • Aligns seller with outcome

Cons / watchouts

  • Seller must trust buyer
  • Terms matter (standby/subordination)
  • Can complicate negotiations

Fix-first

  • Draft terms early (rate, term, standby)
  • Tie to reps/warranties and retrade mechanics
  • Explain how it protects both sides
