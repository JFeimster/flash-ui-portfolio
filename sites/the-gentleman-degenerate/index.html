The house rules

Standards high.
Ego optional.

A few principles for staying ambitious without becoming a bore about it.

  1. 01

    No purity theater.

    We are not trying to become saints. We are trying to become useful, attractive, and consistent.

  2. 02

    Keep your word.

    Discipline is a set of promises kept when nobody is watching.

  3. 03

    Build a life, not a brand.

    Good taste is nice. Good character is better. Brag about neither.

  4. 04

    Earn your vice.

    Enjoy the good stuff with a little sense. Stewardship beats performance.
