# Recommended Pages & Project Structure

## Multi-Step Partner Application
> A high-friction, prestige-driven qualification flow designed to filter serious agency owners. Uses brutalist progress bars, bold questioning, and electric green accents to maintain the punk fintech aesthetic while collecting lead data.

### Suggested Files:
- `ApplicationContainer.tsx`
- `StepIndicator.tsx`
- `QualificationForm.tsx`
- `SuccessLottie.tsx`
- `ApplicationStyles.css`

---

## Partner Command Center (Dashboard)
> The post-login hub for active partners to track their agency's performance. Features high-contrast data visualizations for payouts, lead conversion rates, and a 'Machine Status' panel showing active funding rounds.

### Suggested Files:
- `DashboardLayout.tsx`
- `EarningsOverview.tsx`
- `LeadPipelineTable.tsx`
- `MetricCard.tsx`
- `NotificationSidebar.tsx`

---

## The 'Playbook' Training Portal

### Suggested Files:

- `ResourceCard.tsx`
