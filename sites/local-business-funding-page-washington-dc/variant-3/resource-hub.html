Regulatory Navigation

Compliance Checklist

✓

Basic Business License (BBL)

Required for most businesses in DC. Must be renewed every 2 or 4 years depending on the category.

✓

Clean Hands Certification

A prerequisite for any DC license or grant. Confirms you do not owe more than $100 to the District government.

✓

Registered Agent Requirement

Every DC entity must maintain a registered agent with a physical address within the District (not a P.O. Box).

✓

CBE Certification

Certified Business Enterprises get preference in DC Government contracting—a massive opportunity for local firms.
