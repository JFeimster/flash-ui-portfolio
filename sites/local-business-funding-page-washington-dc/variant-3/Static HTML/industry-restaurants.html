Hospitality Funding FAQ

Can I use funds for a District liquor license?

Absolutely. We specialize in providing the immediate capital required for license acquisition, transfers, and the associated legal fees unique to DC’s ABRA requirements.

How fast can we get funded for an emergency repair?

We know a broken walk-in or HVAC can shut you down. Our "Fast-Track" hospitality applications can result in a wire to your account in as little as 4 to 6 hours.

Do you require personal collateral?

No. Most of our restaurant programs are unsecured, meaning we focus on your establishment's cash flow and health rather than requiring you to leverage your home.

What if we have seasonal dips?

Our revenue-based programs are designed for the DC cycle. Your payments adjust based on your daily sales, easing the burden during slower months when Congress is out of session.
