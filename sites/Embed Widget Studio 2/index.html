Troubleshooting & Support

            

                

                    
Widget is cut off?

                    
Adjust the "Height" parameter in the configuration panel. Most widgets require at least 500px to display correctly on mobile.

                

                

                    
Tracking isn't showing?

                    
Ensure the UTM tracking source field doesn't contain spaces or special characters. Use underscores instead.

                

                

                    
SSL/Security Errors?

                    
All Moonshine widgets are served via HTTPS. Ensure your parent site is also using HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocks.

                

                

                    
Styling doesn't match?

                    
Use the Brand Color picker to align the primary buttons and accents with your partner site's UI.
