The Veteran Jester didn't start in a newsroom or a creative agency. It started in a plywood shack in Kandahar, where the only thing thinner than the coffee was the logic behind the morning briefing.


After two decades of navigating the intersection of high-stakes missions and mind-numbing bureaucracy, the Jester realized that truth is rarely found in the official report. It’s found in the dark humor shared between professionals who know the mission is vital, even when the leadership is completely incompetent.

The Mission Statement

"We live in an age where 'The Truth' has been outsourced to committees and algorithms. The Veteran Jester Dispatch exists to reclaim the human element—the grit, the laughter, and the uncomfortable honesty that comes from having skin in the game."

  • Slaying Sacred Cows: If it’s a buzzword, a fake virtue, or a corporate platitude, we aim for the neck.
  • Translating the Tactical: Taking hard-earned lessons from the field and applying them to the chaos of civilian life and business.
  • Gallows Humor as Armor: Providing a sanctuary for people who use humor to stay sane in an insane world.
  • Unfiltered Intel: No advertisers to appease, no algorithms to chase. Just the ink as it dries.
Operational History

Since leaving active service in 2018, the Jester has consulted for startups, built small businesses, and watched the world increasingly resemble a badly written field manual. The Dispatch was launched as a way to send flares up for those still looking for clarity in the smoke.


This isn't just a blog. It’s a decentralized rally point. We aren't here to tell you what to think, but to remind you that you’re still allowed to think for yourself.
