Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are these calculators?

Our tools use current market benchmark rates and standard banking formulas. However, actual offers depend on your specific credit profile and lender criteria.

Do I need to provide personal info to use them?

No. These tools are 100% free and anonymous. We believe in providing value before asking for your data.

What is a Factor Rate vs. APR?

Factor rates are commonly used in MCA and are expressed as a multiplier (e.g., 1.2). APR includes all fees and interest calculated annually. Our calculators help you bridge that gap.
