Readiness FAQ Library

Why does a personal FICO score affect business funding?

Even for direct corporate loans, lenders assess historical personal repayment patterns as a benchmark for cash management and execution behavior during early operational phases.

Why is a personal mix account rejected immediately?

Enterprise desks use strict programmatic algorithms (like Plaid / DecisionLogic) to analyze business balances. Personal-mix ledgers hide authentic entity operational health, forcing straight rejection.

What impact does deposit frequency have on capital?

Revenue-based underwriters prefer a consistent stream of customer collections. Spreading revenues over 10+ separate clearing deposits per month presents far less programmatic default risk than a single heavy end-of-month check.
