MY STORY: FROM GRIT TO GROWTH

My journey began where resilience is forged: as a combat veteran serving my country. That experience instilled in me a relentless drive and an unwavering belief in empowering the underdog. I saw firsthand the struggle for financial autonomy, especially for those carving their own path. Now, I leverage that same grit in the fintech world.

The modern landscape is rapidly changing. With over a third of the US workforce now engaged in independent work, the gig economy is a powerhouse, projected to encompass over half of the US workforce by 2025. Yet, traditional financial institutions often fail these innovators. They struggle with inconsistent income, securing loans, and building business credit – issues that create unnecessary barriers to growth.

That's where I come in. As an AI & ML scientist and the founder of Moonshine Capital, I'm obsessed with creating cutting-edge solutions. My mission is simple: build an AI-powered ecosystem that gives independent hustlers, YouTubers, brands, and indie founders the financial tools they need to thrive, without sacrificing equity. I turn complex financial challenges into clear, actionable playbooks. I don't just talk about innovation; I build the systems that deliver real freedom and real capital.
