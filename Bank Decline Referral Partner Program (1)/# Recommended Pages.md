\# Recommended Pages 



\## Referral Intake Portal

A secure, streamlined multi-step form designed for bankers to quickly submit client information. It includes fields for 'Reason for Bank Decline' and 'Urgency Level' to ensure the underwriting team prioritizes the lead correctly while maintaining a professional look and feel consistent with the main brand.



\### Suggested Structure:

* referral-portal.html
* portal-styles.css
* form-validation.js
* api-submission-handler.js



\## Partner Performance Dashboard

A private authenticated area for bank managers to track the status of their referred clients in real-time. It features a high-level summary of funding progress (e.g., 'In Underwriting', 'Term Sheet Issued', 'Funded') without violating client privacy, helping the banker see the tangible value they are providing to their clients.



\### Suggested Structure:

* dashboard.html
* dashboard-layout.css
* data-visualizer.js
* auth-middleware.js



\## Partner Toolkit \& Marketing Assets

A resource library where bankers can download white-labeled PDF flyers, access email templates for 'The Warm Introduction', and generate unique QR codes for their desks. This page empowers bankers to facilitate referrals naturally within their existing workflow.



\### Suggested Structure:

* toolkit.html
* resource-cards.css
* asset-generator.js
* download-utility.js



\## Case Study \& Appetite Guide

A detailed educational page showcasing specific scenarios where Moonshine Capital provided funding after a bank decline. It categorizes 'Appetite' by industry and specific hurdle (e.g., low FICO, tax liens, or lack of collateral), giving bankers clarity on which clients are the best fit for the program.



\### Suggested Structure:

* appetite-guide.html
* case-studies.css
* industry-filter.js
* success-stories.json



\## Compliance \& Security Overview

A dedicated credibility page that outlines Moonshine Capital's data encryption standards, privacy policies, and 'Non-Compete' promise. This page is critical for bank managers who need to ensure that referring a client won't result in the client being poached for traditional banking services by third parties.



\### Suggested Structure:

* security-compliance.html
* legal-styles.css
* certificate-display.js
