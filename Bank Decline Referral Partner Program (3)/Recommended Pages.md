**Recommended Pages** 



**Partner Registration \& Onboarding**

A secure portal for bankers to formalize their partnership with Moonshine Capital. This page handles the collection of professional credentials, acceptance of referral terms, and the generation of a unique tracking ID/link for the banker.



Suggested Structure:

* onboarding.html
* registration-form.js
* compliance-terms.pdf
* onboarding-styles.css


**Partner Impact Dashboard**

A private, authenticated interface where bank managers can track the status of their referred clients. It provides real-time updates on whether a client is in 'Review', 'Offer Extended', or 'Funded' status, helping the banker maintain a high-touch relationship without manual follow-up.



Suggested Structure:

* dashboard.html
* referral-status-table.js
* impact-metrics.css
* api-service.js


**Resource \& Marketing Toolkit**

A repository of white-labeled materials for bankers to use in-branch. This includes downloadable PDFs of the referral handout, email templates for introducing clients to Moonshine, and a guide on 'How to Identify an Alternative Finance Candidate'.



Suggested Structure:

* resources.html
* asset-library.js
* referral-handout-template.pdf
* toolkit-ui.css


**Product Matrix \& Underwriting Guide**

A deep-dive technical page specifically for bankers that outlines the specific funding paths available (e.g., Asset-Based Lending, Factoring, Revenue-Based Financing). It provides transparency on credit requirements, time-in-business minimums, and industry exclusions to ensure high-quality referrals.



Suggested Structure:

* guidelines.html
* product-matrix.css
* underwriting-logic.js
* funding-paths-chart.svg


**Success Stories \& Client Path Gallery**

A library of anonymized case studies highlighting clients who were declined by traditional banks but successfully funded through Moonshine Capital. Each story focuses on the 'Banker's perspective'—how the referral preserved the client relationship and eventually brought a more 'bankable' client back to the institution later.



Suggested Structure:

* case-studies.html
* success-stories.css
* story-filter.js
* narrative-layout.css
