**Recommended Pages** 



Partner Dashboard

A secure portal for bank managers to monitor the status of their referrals. It provides a real-time view of which stage a client is in (Application, Review, Funded) without disclosing sensitive financial data, ensuring the banker stays informed of the client's progress.



Suggested Structure:

dashboard.html

dashboard-styles.css

referral-table.js

status-tracking-api.js

analytics-charts.js



Funding Solutions Catalog

An educational resource page for bankers detailing the specific alternative funding products available (e.g., Revenue-Based Financing, Asset-Based Loans, Bridge Loans). This builds credibility by showing the depth of Moonshine Capital’s options and helps bankers identify which clients are the best fit.



Suggested Structure:

solutions.html

product-cards.css

funding-logic.js

product-comparison-matrix.html



Partner Onboarding \& Agreement

A streamlined registration flow where bankers can officially join the referral program. It includes the digital signature for the referral agreement, profile setup, and the generation of their unique referral ID and custom QR code.



Suggested Structure:

signup.html

onboarding-flow.js

terms-agreement.html

qr-code-generator.js

auth-styles.css



Marketing Resource Toolkit

A centralized hub where bank managers can download branded materials to facilitate the referral process. This includes the PDF referral handout mentioned in the prompt, email templates for warm introductions, and digital signage for internal bank use.



Suggested Structure:

resources.html

resource-grid.css

template-preview.js

download-manager.js

assets/referral-handout-template.pdf



Client Intake Application

The specific landing page for the referred business owner. It maintains the Moonshine Capital branding but includes a 'Referred by \[Banker Name]' header to create a warm handoff. It features a simplified multi-step form to capture essential business data quickly.



Suggested Structure:

client-intake.html

multi-step-form.js

form-validation.js

intake-styles.css

confirmation-page.html
