Common Questions

Can I get a business loan with no revenue? +

Yes, via personal credit stacking or secured loans. Traditional business loans usually require revenue, but these early-stage alternatives focus on personal creditworthiness or assets.

Will startup funding require a personal guarantee? +

Almost certainly. In the first 2 years, lenders look to the founder to back the loan since the business doesn't have enough history.
